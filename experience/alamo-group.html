Selected Work

01

Heavy-Duty Fixture Design

Designed a heavy-duty manufacturing fixture intended for use in a production environment. The project required balancing strength, manufacturability, safety, and practical shop-floor use.

02

FEA Validation

Applied FEA to evaluate the fixture under expected loading conditions and identify areas of high stress before fabrication.

The analysis helped support design decisions before the fixture was built and placed into production use.

03

Fabrication Collaboration

Worked directly with fabricators while developing the fixture to make sure the design could be manufactured correctly and would meet the required specifications.

This allowed me to see how CAD and analysis decisions translate into actual fabrication, assembly, and shop-floor use.

04

Robot Welding Investigation

Another intern and I were assigned to determine why robotic welding stations repeatedly needed to be reprogrammed when new workpieces arrived at their stations.

After observing the welding operation and discussing the issue with the operators, we determined that the robotic welders were responding to dimensional inconsistencies in the incoming parts rather than creating the problem themselves.

05

Root Cause Investigation

We followed the production flow upstream and traced the dimensional variation back to the brake press operation.

The investigation showed that some operators were learning the process while working, machine calibration was not always consistent, and bend angles could vary throughout the day.

Those inconsistencies were then carried downstream to the robotic welding stations, where additional programming adjustments were needed to compensate for the variation.

06

Brake Press Kaizen Event

The investigation contributed to a Kaizen event focused on improving the brake press process, which was a bottleneck in the production flow.

The goal was to reduce defects, improve bend consistency, strengthen the process used by operators, and reduce the amount of downstream adjustment required at the robotic welding stations.

07

Manufacturing Process Improvement

The project showed how a production problem appearing at one station can originate much earlier in the manufacturing process.

By observing the operators, following the workflow, and identifying the source of variation, we were able to focus improvement efforts on the process creating the inconsistency rather than only correcting the problem downstream.

08

Project Management

Used available resources, operator feedback, engineering tools, and project planning to complete assigned projects within designated deadlines.

The internship strengthened my understanding of how engineering, manufacturing, fabrication, and production teams work together to solve practical problems.
