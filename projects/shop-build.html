PROJECT

40 × 60 Steel Shop Build

Ground-up design, structural analysis, fabrication, construction coordination, electrical planning, and shop-layout development for a 40 × 60 × 18 ft steel building.

40 × 60 × 18 ft Structural Design & Fabrication SolidWorks + FEA

Overview

This project involved designing and building a 40 × 60 × 18 ft steel shop from the ground up. I began the project in SolidWorks, using an existing smaller shop on the property as a practical reference while developing a larger structure for fabrication, machining, vehicle work, storage, and future equipment expansion.

The project required structural loading, material sizing, foundation coordination, steel fabrication, construction sequencing, electrical requirements, three-phase power conversion, and shop-floor organization.

Project Scope

Took the building from early CAD design through structural analysis, concrete coordination, fabrication, assembly, electrical planning, and operational shop improvements.

Selected Work

01

SolidWorks Building Design

Developed the overall building concept in SolidWorks before construction, using an existing smaller shop on the property as a reference point.

The CAD model helped establish overall dimensions, structural layout, member placement, usable interior space, and the relationship between the building structure and future shop equipment.

02

Structural Analysis & Material Sizing

Used FEA and engineering calculations to evaluate the structure and help select material sizes capable of resisting expected loading.

The analysis considered wind loads, snow loads, structural weight, and other natural forces acting on the building.

03

Foundation Coordination

Coordinated with concrete contractors to make sure the foundation was prepared for the steel structure and that embedded steel plates were located correctly before concrete placement was completed.

Accurate plate placement was important because the steel frame depended on those locations for alignment during fabrication and erection.

04

Steel Fabrication & Assembly

Fabricated the steel structure and coordinated the project through construction and assembly.

This required translating the CAD design into real structural members, welded assemblies, connection points, and an erection sequence that could be completed safely and efficiently.

05

Project Coordination

Coordinated design, concrete work, structural fabrication, building assembly, electrical planning, and shop layout.

The project required adjusting construction decisions around contractor schedules, fabrication progress, material availability, and the final operational needs of the shop.

06

Electrical & 3-Phase Equipment Planning

Worked within electrical requirements and installation rules while planning power distribution for the shop.

Added a three-phase converter so the building could support industrial equipment requiring three-phase power, including lathes, mills, and CNC milling equipment.

07

Shop Layout & Equipment Use

Planned the interior around practical fabrication and machining operations, allowing room for equipment, vehicle work, material handling, and future expansion.

The shop was designed to function as an active work environment rather than simply as storage space.

08

Heavy-Duty Vertical Storage

Added heavy-duty shelving capable of storing palletized materials vertically, reducing the amount of valuable floor space used for storage.

The storage system increased usable shop space while keeping materials organized and accessible for fabrication and service work.

Engineering Takeaway

This project gave me experience connecting engineering design with construction and real-world fabrication. Decisions made in SolidWorks had to work with concrete placement, steel fabrication, field assembly, electrical requirements, equipment needs, and the day-to-day operation of the finished shop.

It strengthened my understanding of structural design, practical manufacturability, project coordination, and the importance of designing around how a system will actually be built and used.
