SENIOR DESIGN · WORK IN PROGRESS

TXST T-Shirt Launcher

Senior Design project for Texas State University focused on developing a safe, repeatable, and engaging T-shirt launcher for athletic and campus events.

Senior Design Team Member 2026–2027 Product Development In Development

Overview

This project is currently in development. My role is to help guide the team through customer-needs research, stakeholder interviews, technical evaluation, and product-development decisions while keeping schedule, feasibility, and budget in mind.

As the project moves into concept generation and detailed design, I will use my background in mechanical systems, fabrication, CAD, and hands-on problem solving to help determine which concepts can realistically be manufactured, assembled, tested, and delivered as a complete working system.

My Responsibilities

  • Lead and support stakeholder interviews, customer-needs research, and data collection
  • Translate stakeholder input into clear engineering requirements and design constraints
  • Evaluate concepts for technical feasibility, safety, manufacturability, and fabrication practicality
  • Help lead product-design decisions as the project moves from research into concept development
  • Coordinate design, analysis, fabrication, testing, and team contributions into one complete system
  • Track project progress, deadlines, deliverables, and design decisions with the available budget in mind

Tools & Skills

SolidWorks Customer Needs Stakeholder Interviews Requirements Development Product Design Concept Evaluation Mechanical Systems Fabrication Project Coordination Testing Safety

Current Work

01

Stakeholder Needs

Helping lead interviews and data collection with project stakeholders to understand how the launcher will be used, what operators need, and which performance, safety, mobility, capacity, and crowd-engagement requirements matter most.

02

Requirements & Technical Understanding

Working with the team to turn raw stakeholder input into clear engineering requirements while developing a deeper understanding of the pneumatic, mechanical, safety, and fabrication considerations that will influence the final design.

03

Concept Feasibility

During concept generation, I will help evaluate ideas based on whether they can realistically be designed, fabricated, assembled, operated, and tested within the project's available resources, schedule, and constraints.

04

Product Design & Team Integration

As the project progresses, I will help lead product-design decisions and make sure the work from different team members comes together into a complete system rather than separate parts. This includes keeping design, analysis, fabrication, testing, and project planning aligned.

Project Status

The team is currently completing the early product-development stages, including stakeholder research, customer-needs development, benchmarking, and preparation for concept generation. The page will continue to be updated as the project moves into concept selection, CAD development, analysis, fabrication, prototyping, and testing.
